RAPID REDISTRIBUTION PIPELINE

How Annapoorna Bridge Works

Designed specifically for Indian wedding buffets, catering banquets, and campus messes where surplus food is hot, perishable, and needs safe redistribution within hours.

1

Donor Posts Surplus

Event manager or hostel supervisor inputs estimated portion count, cooked timestamp, and accepts mandatory FSSAI food safety checklist.

2

Local NGO Claims & Dispatches

Nearby verified NGOs receive instant push alerts. One-click claim assigns pick-up volunteers and generates digital dispatch slip with donor contact and OTP.

3

Safe Distribution & Audit

Courier verifies handoff via OTP, maintains covered transit, and delivers food to shelters or community kitchens before the safe window expires.

GLOBAL GOALS FOR SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

Targeting UN SDG 2 & SDG 11

India wastes an estimated 68 million tonnes of food annually while millions experience nutritional insufficiency. Annapoorna Bridge operationalizes SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) and SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities) by transforming post-event waste into community sustenance while cutting greenhouse gas emissions.

  • 🌾 SDG 2.1: Universal access to safe and nutritious food.
  • 🏙️ SDG 11.6: Reducing per-capita urban environmental impact by diverting landfill waste.
  • 📜 FSSAI 2019: Full compliance with national surplus food recovery protocols.
