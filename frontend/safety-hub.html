🚚 Safe Transit Standard Operating Procedures for NGOs

1. Vehicle Sanitization

Vehicles used for food recovery must be free from dust, fuel residue, and maintained in clean hygienic condition.

2. Covered Thermal Containers

All vessels must be securely lidded. Hot food should be insulated to retain temperature above 60°C during transport.

3. Immediate Community Serving

Food should be served immediately upon reaching destination shelters; avoid secondary reheating cycles.
