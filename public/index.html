FSSAI Surplus Food Handling & Safety Hub

Surplus food redistribution requires uncompromising temperature discipline and hygienic containers. All donors and NGOs on Annapoorna Bridge must adhere to these guidelines.

1. The Temperature Danger Zone (5°C to 60°C)

Bacteria grow most rapidly in the range between 5°C and 60°C, doubling in number in as little as 20 minutes. Keep hot food piping hot, and chilled food ice cold.

❄️ Cold Preservation < 5°C

Keep dairy, salads, and desserts in commercial chillers until pickup.

⚠️ Danger Zone 5°C - 60°C

Maximum 4 hours exposure! Any cooked food in this zone must be consumed within 4 hours.

🔥 Hot Holding > 60°C

Keep curries, gravies, and biryanis in insulated thermal containers or bain-marie.

2. Prohibited Foods for Redistribution

Raw Seafood & Uncooked Meat: Extremely high spoilage risk; strictly forbidden from redistribution.
Cut Raw Fruits & Pre-mixed Salads: Salads containing mayonnaise, yogurt, or raw dressings past 2 hours.
Reheated Food Leftovers: Food that has already been reheated multiple times cannot be donated.
Swollen / Damaged Canned Goods: Any packaged items exhibiting bloating, rust, or puncture leaks.

3. Transport Hygiene Protocols for NGOs & Volunteers

  • Use food-grade stainless steel containers with airtight or securely latched lids.
  • Ensure transport vehicles are clean, sheltered from rain and direct sunlight, and sanitized daily.
  • Always verify the 6-character Pickup Verification Code at the venue before loading.

FSSAI Surplus Food Guidelines

1. Golden 4-Hour Rule: Cooked food held between 5°C and 60°C must be distributed and consumed within 4 hours of cooking.

2. Temperature Maintenance: Hot foods must be stored at >60°C in food-grade insulated warmers. Cold desserts and dairy must be maintained below 5°C.

3. Sensory Inspection: Donors and NGOs must visually inspect food for discoloration, abnormal odor, or texture souring prior to handover.

4. Vessel Protocol: Only clean, sanitized stainless steel vessels or food-grade sealed cartons can be used for transit.
